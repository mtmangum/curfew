# Saved progress and causal retry — 2026-10-05

A7 is implemented locally with A4–A6, after the released A3 baseline. A completed
walk earns the next level across visits, and retry feedback distinguishes capture
from depleted life. All follow-ups remain uncommitted and undeployed.

## Resulting behavior

- `Start.tscn` is the entry scene. A first visit starts level 1 directly; a returning
  player gets Continue at the highest unlocked level and New Run at level 1. New
  Run retains unlocked levels and rule memory. Both choices support pointer and
  keyboard input, visible focus, and readable portrait/landscape/desktop layouts.
- The chooser creates no city before selection. Its loading message stays over
  the chosen game's progressive build, then it is released. That game becomes the
  current scene, so normal retries reload Main directly instead of asking again.
  The Web loader reveals the chooser without claiming a city is built; selecting
  a walk has no hidden loading-tip hold.
- `Progress.gd` stores version 1 and the highest unlocked level in localStorage
  `curfew_progress` on Web, or `user://progress.json` natively. A normal clear saves
  `completed + 1`; lower clears cannot downgrade an unlock. No mid-chase world or
  cross-device state is saved. R/retry still retains the current house, map and a
  nearby respawn position in this session. LEVEL/CURFEW_LEVEL debug selections and
  fixture overrides do not earn saved unlocks.
- Missing, malformed, unsupported, fractional and out-of-range saves fall back to
  level 1. Denied reads are caught. A failed write retains the in-session unlock
  and adds “Progress is kept for this session only” to the win screen. A later
  visit without readable storage safely starts at level 1.
- Capture says that a cop reached Nicole, life does not stop capture, and walking
  away/breaking sight is the response; Sneak is too slow during a chase. Depleted
  life names a car hit (including Stella), skateboarder, punk, zombie or hobo grip,
  with a crossing/avoidance response and pizza recovery where appropriate. Generic
  life loss also has a fallback explanation. Retry stays quick and optional to read.
