Level guide

Five named levels, with a fresh neighbourhood generated for each new level. Clear a level to continue; later levels build on the previous one's challenges.

  1. Level 1

    Past Curfew

    A gentler walk home: fewer, less alert cops, light traffic, and no hobos, punks, or zombies. Working phone booths give directions; squirrels distract Stella. Home is closer and Stella offers scent hints more often.

    Warm night · Clear skies · Working streetlights

  2. Level 2

    Cold Nose

    Full patrols, hobos, punks, and zombies, with much heavier traffic. Lingering attracts zombies. Phone booths stop working and squirrels disappear. Home is farther away and scent hints are less frequent.

    Cold teal light · Fog · Blackout · Wind and distant sirens

  3. Level 3

    Lights Out

    Adds rain, puddles, and thunder, and the dark: the world is nearly black except where a street light, a fire, a cop's torch, or Nicole's own torch (F) lights it. Rain reduces how far noise carries. Cops become more alert and the minimum distance home increases, but the roads are quieter: the dark is enough to deal with. Police cars cruise the roads and chase along them; when one reaches the kerb a cop gets out and runs at Nicole. Stella's scent hints become less frequent again.

    Rain and thunder · Thicker fog · Near-total darkness, lit by lamps and torches

  4. Level 4

    The Pound

    The neighbourhood looks abandoned: boarded windows, graffiti, wrecked cars, and more quarantine barricades. Cop alertness, fog, rain, and the minimum distance home increase further, and the roads stay quiet.

    Boarded buildings · Wrecked cars · More barricades

  5. Level 5

    Homeward Hound

    Keeps the abandoned, rainy city and pushes the challenge further: more skateboarders, sharper cops, and a longer minimum distance home.

    Abandoned city · Heavier rain · A longer journey

  6. Level 6 onward

    The night continues

    There is no final level. These levels keep the “Homeward Hound” title and the same theme. Difficulty increases until each setting reaches its cap; new neighbourhoods keep the route changing.

    Same theme · Difficulty eventually caps · Endless progression

Original sprite assets and code-drawn previews, before the game's lighting, fog, and camera zoom. The rooftop, street-detail, and signal previews are rendered from Godot's own drawing code; other buildings, vehicles, and scenery are drawn in-game.
